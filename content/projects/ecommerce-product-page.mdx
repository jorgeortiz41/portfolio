## Context

A deliberately scoped front-end exercise: one page, done properly, rather than a
half-finished application.

## Approach

Built responsive-first with Tailwind, focusing on the details that separate a
product page that works from one that merely renders — image gallery behaviour,
quantity and cart interaction, and layout that holds up from phone to desktop
without a separate mobile tree.
